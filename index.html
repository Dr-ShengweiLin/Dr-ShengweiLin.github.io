关于我About

我是上海长海医院的副主任医师，研究方向为脓毒症和围术期器官保护。我的工作从基础研究出发，关注巨噬细胞代谢与炎症调控；同时也利用临床队列和重症数据库，研究危重患者的器官损伤、术后谵妄和用药风险。I am an Associate Chief Physician at Changhai Hospital in Shanghai, working on sepsis and perioperative organ protection. My work spans basic research on macrophage metabolism and inflammation, and clinical cohort and critical care database studies on organ injury, postoperative delirium and medication risk.

研究方向Research

四个方向，围绕同一个问题：如何让危重和手术患者的器官更安全。Four themes around one question: how to keep organs safer in critically ill and surgical patients.

  • 脓毒症的免疫与代谢调控Immunity and metabolism in sepsis

    研究巨噬细胞中 HIF-1α 依赖的糖酵解如何影响内毒素血症炎症反应，以及利多卡因、哇巴因等药物的保护机制；参与了固有免疫调控分子 IRAK-M 和 Clec18a 的研究。How HIF-1α-dependent glycolysis in macrophages shapes endotoxemia, and how drugs such as lidocaine and ouabain protect against it; also contributed to work on the innate immune regulators IRAK-M and Clec18a.

  • 围术期与重症器官保护Organ protection in surgery and critical care

    利用 MIMIC-IV 数据库，研究急性胰腺炎患者急性肾损伤的预测，以及阿片类药物剂量与呼吸机相关性肺炎的关系。Using MIMIC-IV to predict acute kidney injury in acute pancreatitis, and to study how opioid dose relates to ventilator-associated pneumonia.

  • 术后谵妄的生物标志物Biomarkers of postoperative delirium

    探索全身炎症反应指数（SIRI）和纤维蛋白原白蛋白比值（FAR）等简便指标，能否在术前识别谵妄高风险患者。Whether simple indices such as the systemic inflammation response index (SIRI) and the fibrinogen-to-albumin ratio (FAR) can flag patients at high risk of delirium before surgery.

  • 重症数据科学Critical care data science

    应用机器学习、倾向评分匹配和限制性立方样条等方法，从真实世界数据中建立预测模型并检验剂量反应关系。Machine learning, propensity score matching and restricted cubic splines to build prediction models and test dose-response relationships in real-world data.
